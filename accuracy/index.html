How this is worked out

The forecast. The clock averages six weather models (ECMWF, GFS, ICON, GEM, UK Met Office and Météo-France). "Chance of rain" is the share of the six that expect rain in an hour. This page takes each model's forecast as it was issued the day before, so it is the same method the clock uses.

"Take an umbrella" means half or more of the models agreed on rain at some point, or about 1 mm or more was forecast. "Maybe" means a third or more, or about 0.3 mm. A wet day here means 1 mm or more fell.

What actually happened. This is a computer reconstruction of the weather (called ERA5, from Open-Meteo's free archive), not a rain gauge or thermometer in the yard. It is very good for temperature and fair for rain. Light showers of a few tenths of a millimetre are the hardest to judge, so treat small differences with care.

Not covered. The Bureau of Meteorology's own forecast and observations are not used here.
